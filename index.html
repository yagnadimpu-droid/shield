2. Operations Research Mathematical Formulation

Objective Function (Cost Minimization):

Minimize Z = ∑ (c_dist * Distance_ij) + ∑ (c_fuel * Fuel_ij) + ∑ (c_delay * max(0, Arrival_i - Latest_i)) + ∑ (c_rebal * Rebalance_Depot)

Subject to Hard Operational Constraints:

  • Payload Capacity Bound: Total payload weight ∑ w_i ≤ Vehicle Capacity W_max and volume ∑ v_i ≤ V_max at all times.
  • Customer Delivery Time Windows: Arrival time A_i satisfies Earliest_i ≤ A_i ≤ Latest_i. Early arrivals wait; late arrivals incur heavy penalties.
  • Driver Hours of Service (HOS): Cumulative driving + service time T_shift ≤ 10.0 hours (600 minutes) per shift.
  • Asset Operational Readiness: Vehicle state == ACTIVE. Broken or telemetry-quarantined assets are excluded from assignment.
