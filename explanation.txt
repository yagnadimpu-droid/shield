1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PROJECT GOAL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Project Name: SmartFleet (Global Real-Time Delivery Network)
Domain: Road Logistics, Operations Research (OR) & Multi-Scale Fleet Optimization.

Core Philosophy:
"SmartFleet is a REAL-TIME ROAD DELIVERY AND FLEET OPTIMIZATION SYSTEM.
From an Amazon/Flipkart-style local doorstep delivery to an inter-state shipment,
SmartFleet continuously optimizes the vehicle assignment, road traffic, and route."

Scope:
Road Delivery ONLY. No Air, Sea, or Rail. The platform optimizes road delivery fleets
modeled after real-world e-commerce giants (Amazon / Flipkart):
1. Last-Mile Doorstep Delivery:
   Fulfillment Center (FC) / Sortation Delivery Station ➔ Urban Delivery Van / 2-Wheeler ➔ Customer Apartment / Residence Doorstep.
2. Same-Day City Express Delivery:
   Intra-City delivery between commercial hubs, residential clusters, and tech parks.
3. Twin-City Road Transport:
   Cross-city arterial logistics (e.g. Hyderabad ➔ Secunderabad).
4. Inter-City Trunk Route:
   Regional highway distribution connecting major cities (e.g. Hyderabad ➔ Warangal).
5. Inter-State Linehaul:
   Heavy freight haulers on National Highways (e.g. Telangana ➔ Maharashtra via NH65).
6. Continental Cross-Country Highways:
   Major highway logistics (e.g. Frankfurt ➔ Paris via A6/A4, New York ➔ Chicago via I-80).

The Solution:
SmartFleet combines real road-following routes, continuous physical coordinate
interpolation without teleportation, dynamic traffic jam detection and AI rerouting,
and sub-150ms mathematical optimization (Google OR-Tools VRPTW).

2. FRONTEND ARCHITECTURE & WHAT IS VISIBLE ON SCREEN
================================================================================

When you open http://localhost:5000, you are presented with the Global Real-Time Delivery
Network dashboard:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. TOP GLOBAL MAP CONTROL BAR (SINGLE-STRIP, NON-OBSTRUCTIVE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Positioned neatly at the very top of the map in a single horizontal strip (never wrapping
into the middle of the screen):

1. Scale Navigation Presets (Amazon / Flipkart Delivery Hierarchy):
   - [🌍 World]: Global 3D view showing worldwide road distribution hubs.
   - [🇮🇳 National]: National highway grid across major metropolitan centers.
   - [🛣️ Inter-State]: Inter-state linehaul corridors (e.g. Hyderabad ➔ Mumbai NH65).
   - [🏙️ City Hub]: City delivery network (e.g. Hyderabad urban road network).
   - [📍 Doorstep]: High-precision zoom right onto the customer apartment / doorstep.

2. E-Commerce Scenarios & Traffic Optimization Buttons:
   - [⚡ Amazon/Flipkart Delivery]: Demonstrates an Amazon/Flipkart last-mile dispatch:
     Order #2048 arrives at Amazon HYD1 Delivery Station (85 kg). SmartFleet validates
     vehicle capacity, driver availability, and time window (10:00–11:00 AM).
     It assigns Vehicle V-17, zooms into the road network, and starts moving along
     real street turns to the customer doorstep.
   - [🚦 Dynamic Traffic Reroute]: Simulates real-time traffic optimization.
     Detects a severe traffic jam at the Cyber Towers Flyover (+25 min delay).
     SmartFleet re-optimizes the route in 85ms and diverts the vehicle onto the
     clear Outer Ring Road bypass, saving 22 minutes!

3. Simulation Controls & Basemap:
   - [▶ Live / Pause]: Toggles vehicle movement.
   - [↻ Reset]: Resets vehicles to start.
   - Speed Selector: 1x, 2x, 5x warp speed.
   - Basemap Dropdown: Cyber Dark Ops, Google Roads, Satellite, Traffic, 3D Globe.
   - [📊 Ops HUD] Button: Shows or hides the Operations HUD card anytime with one click.

C. REAL ROAD-FOLLOWING ROUTES & VEHICLE MOVEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Real Road Geometry: Routes physically steer along roads, highways, and street turns
  (no straight lines, no random jumping):
  * Amazon/Flipkart Last-Mile: Amazon HYD1 Delivery Station ➔ JNTU Main Road ➔ Forum Mall ➔ Cyber Towers Incline ➔ Madhapur 100ft Road ➔ Customer Apartment Gate.
  * City Express: Kukatpally ➔ Cyber Gateway ➔ Mindspace Circle ➔ Bio-Diversity ➔ Gachibowli Financial District.
  * Inter-State Linehaul: NH65 Patancheru ➔ Zaheerabad ➔ Solapur ➔ Pune ➔ Mumbai-Pune Expressway ➔ Mumbai.
- Persistent Marker Engine: Vehicle coordinates glide smoothly along road waypoints
  every 100ms. State polling does NOT destroy or reset markers, preventing erratic jumps.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D. DYNAMIC ROUTE TRAFFIC OPTIMIZATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Traffic Bottleneck Detection:
   - Displays real-time highway bottleneck at Cyber Towers Flyover Junction (in glowing red).
   - Direct path travel time: 38 min (Heavy traffic congestion).
2. Automated Alternative Bypass:
   - SmartFleet calculates the alternative arterial bypass via the Outer Ring Road.
   - Clear bypass travel time: 16 min (Free flow).
   - Clicking [🚦 Dynamic Traffic Reroute] dynamically switches the route, redirects the
     vehicle marker, updates ETA from 38 min to 16 min, and logs the re-optimization.
